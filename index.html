BEFORE YOU POP IN

A few little
things to know.

How do I book an appointment?+

Tap any booking button, choose the service you’re interested in, and continue to WhatsApp. Send your message and we’ll discuss availability with you. Your appointment is confirmed only when the salon replies to confirm it.

Where can I see services and prices?+

Message us on WhatsApp for the current service menu and pricing. Share what you’re looking for so we can help you plan your visit.

Can I bring inspiration photos?+

Absolutely. Send your inspiration on WhatsApp or bring it along. It’s a lovely way to start a conversation about the look you have in mind.

How do I change my appointment?+

Please message us on WhatsApp or call 018-328 2070 as early as you can. Include your name and your original appointment date so we can help find another time.

YOUR PRIVACY

Simple, by design.

This website does not use analytics, advertising cookies, or store your booking details. The booking form prepares a WhatsApp message containing the details you enter.

When you continue to WhatsApp, that information is included in the link sent to WhatsApp. Messages you choose to send are handled by WhatsApp and the salon. Instagram and Google Maps are external services with their own privacy policies.

To ask about information shared with the salon, please call 018-328 2070.
